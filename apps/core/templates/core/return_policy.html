Return & Refund Policy

⚠️ This is a starter template. Please review and update with your actual return policy before going live.

Last updated: 2026

Return Window

We accept return and exchange requests within 14 days of delivery.

Eligibility Conditions

To be eligible for a return or exchange, items must be:

  • Unused and unworn
  • In their original condition
  • With original tags and packaging intact
  • Free from signs of use, damage, or alteration

Defective or Damaged Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item, please contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We will prioritize a resolution for you.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

  • Registered customers: Log in → My Orders → Select Order → Request Return/Exchange
  • Guest customers: Visit our Returns page and enter your Order Number and Email/Phone

Refunds

Once your return is approved and received, we will process your refund. Refund timelines may vary depending on your payment method.

Order Cancellations

Orders can be self-cancelled while in Pending status. Once confirmed or shipped, please contact our support team.

Contact

For return queries, contact us at support@kesslerandhyde.com.
