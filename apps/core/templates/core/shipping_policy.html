Delivery Coverage

We currently deliver nationwide across Pakistan.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are calculated at checkout based on your order total:

  • Orders below the free shipping threshold — a flat delivery fee applies
  • Orders at or above the free shipping threshold — Free Delivery

Current delivery charges and free shipping threshold are shown at checkout.

Estimated Delivery Time

Orders are typically delivered within 3–5 business days after confirmation. Delivery times may vary depending on your location.

Order Processing

  • Orders are processed after confirmation
  • You will receive an email notification when your order is shipped
  • You can track your order status using your order number

Cash on Delivery (COD)

We offer Cash on Delivery. Payment is collected by the delivery person upon receipt of your order.

Contact

For shipping queries, contact us at support@kesslerandhyde.com.
